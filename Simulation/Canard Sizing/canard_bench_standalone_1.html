Constraint margins

The shortest bar decides the design. Raise the linkage ratio before changing the planform — it moves the error floor, torque and divergence together at no aerodynamic cost — but watch the slew-rate margin, which falls as 1/n and is what should stop you.

Hover a bar for what that margin is worth. They are not all the same kind of thing: some are exact, one is a scaling law and one is pure policy.

Bearing coupling

A free bearing (left) leaves the controlled forebody almost undamped in roll — the plant pole approaches the origin and full deflection would run the body up to a far higher steady rate. Seizing the bearing (right) hands the forebody the tail's damping back. What that costs you in closed loop is a question for the MATLAB side; this only shows the plant moving under you.
